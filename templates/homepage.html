Featured Animals

Mort

Mammal · Mouse Lemur · Africa

Mort is a tiny mouse lemur who loves climbing and late-night snacks of fruit and insects.

King Julien

Mammal · Lemur · Africa

Known for his big personality, King Julien spends his day sunbathing and watching over the lemur habitat.

Rich

Reptile · (Species) · North America

Rich is a calm, cold-blooded reptile who enjoys basking under heat lamps and exploring his rocky enclosure.

Sandy Cheeks

Mammal · Flying Squirrel · North America

Sandy is a rescued flying squirrel who now lives safely in a specially designed climbing habitat.
